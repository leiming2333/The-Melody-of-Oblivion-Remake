# 行为准则 / Code of Conduct

简体中文 · [English](#english)

## 我们的承诺

作为社区成员、贡献者和维护者，我们承诺让每个人都能在不受骚扰的环境中参与项目，无论其年龄、体型、可见或不可见的残障、族裔、性征、性别认同与表达、经验、教育、社会经济地位、国籍、外貌、种族、种姓、肤色、宗教、性身份或性取向如何。

我们会以开放、友善、尊重差异和包容的方式交流，共同维护健康的社区。

## 行为标准

我们鼓励以下行为：

- 善意对待他人，体谅不同的背景和经验。
- 尊重不同意见，围绕具体问题讨论。
- 提出并接受建设性的反馈。
- 为自己的错误承担责任，向受影响的人道歉，并从中学习。
- 既考虑个人需求，也顾及整个社区的利益。

以下行为不可接受：

- 使用色情语言或图像，进行不受欢迎的性关注或示好。
- 挑衅、侮辱、贬损，以及人身或政治攻击。
- 公开或私下骚扰、威胁他人，或因他人举报而报复。
- 未经明确许可，公开他人的住址、邮箱、账号凭据或其他私人信息。
- 其他在专业协作环境中可合理认定为不当的行为。

## 维护者的责任

维护者负责解释和执行本准则，对不当、威胁、冒犯或有害行为作出适当、公平的处理。

维护者有权删除、编辑或拒绝违反本准则的评论、提交、代码、Wiki 编辑、Issue、PR 和其他贡献，并在适当时说明理由。

## 适用范围

本准则适用于项目的所有社区空间，包括 Issue、PR、代码审核和其他由项目管理的交流渠道。个人在公共场合正式代表项目时也须遵守本准则，例如使用官方账号发言或作为指定代表参加活动。

## 举报与隐私

如遇辱骂、骚扰或其他不当行为，请通过 [leiming2333@qq.com](mailto:leiming2333@qq.com) 向维护者 [@leiming2333](https://github.com/leiming2333) 私密举报。

请提供事件发生的位置、时间、相关链接或截图，以及可以安全联系你的方式。只提供处理事件所需的信息，不要在公开 Issue 中发布投诉细节或他人的私人信息。

维护者会及时、公平地审查和调查投诉，尊重举报者的隐私与安全，只在处理事件所需的范围内分享报告内容。不得报复善意举报的人。

如果投诉涉及维护者本人，或需要 GitHub 平台介入，可以使用 GitHub 的 [举报滥用行为渠道](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)。安全漏洞请按 [安全政策](SECURITY.md) 报告。

## 处理方针

维护者会根据行为的影响和严重程度决定如何处理：

1. **纠正**：对不恰当的语言或其他不受欢迎的行为，私下书面说明问题及原因，必要时要求公开道歉。
2. **警告**：对单次或多次违规发出警告，说明继续违规的后果，并可要求在指定时间内停止与相关人员互动，包括在社区外主动联系相关人员或执行准则的维护者。违反限制可能导致临时或永久封禁。
3. **临时封禁**：对严重或持续违规，在指定时间内禁止参与社区互动或公开交流。期间不得公开或私下联系事件相关人员，包括主动联系执行准则的维护者。违反限制可能导致永久封禁。
4. **永久封禁**：对持续违规、骚扰个人，或攻击、贬低某类人群的行为，永久禁止在社区内公开互动。

## English

### Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

### Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences, and discussing specific issues.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience.
- Considering the interests of the overall community as well as our own needs.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment, threats, or retaliation against someone for reporting misconduct.
- Publishing another person's private information, such as their address, email, or account credentials, without explicit permission.
- Other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

### Enforcement Responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards, and for taking appropriate and fair corrective action in response to inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful behavior.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, pull requests, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and will communicate moderation reasons when appropriate.

### Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project community spaces, including issues, pull requests, code reviews, and other project-managed channels. It also applies when someone officially represents the project in public, such as posting through an official account or acting as an appointed representative at an event.

### Reporting and Privacy

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior privately to maintainer [@leiming2333](https://github.com/leiming2333) at [leiming2333@qq.com](mailto:leiming2333@qq.com). Include the location and time of the incident, relevant links or screenshots, and a safe way to contact you. Share only information needed to handle the incident; do not post complaint details or another person's private information in a public issue.

Maintainers will review and investigate complaints promptly and fairly, respect reporters' privacy and safety, and limit sharing to what is needed to handle the incident. Retaliation for good-faith reports is prohibited.

If the report concerns the maintainer or requires GitHub's intervention, use GitHub's [abuse reporting channels](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam). Report security vulnerabilities under the [Security Policy](SECURITY.md#english).

### Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers select consequences based on the impact and severity of the behavior:

1. **Correction**: inappropriate language or other unwelcome behavior may receive a private written explanation of the violation and why it was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.
2. **Warning**: a single incident or series of violations may receive a warning with consequences for continued behavior, and a specified period without interaction with those involved. This includes unsolicited contact with maintainers enforcing the code and contact through external channels. Violations may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.
3. **Temporary ban**: serious or sustained violations may result in a specified period without community interaction or public communication. Public or private contact with those involved, including unsolicited contact with maintainers enforcing the code, is prohibited during this period. Violations may lead to a permanent ban.
4. **Permanent ban**: a pattern of violations, harassment of an individual, or aggression toward or disparagement of groups may result in a permanent ban from public interaction within the community.
